Reading these figures

  • Amounts are the appropriations in each year’s GAA, in pesos. The GAA publishes them in thousands.
  • A program is listed when its name includes Iloilo, Ilo-ilo. Names are matched, not places surveyed.
  • A DPWH office’s yearly figure is its whole budget, as the GAA lists it under that office — never split.
  • The 2027 figures are the NEP and the House bill, not law. They are shown beside the enacted years, never added to them.
