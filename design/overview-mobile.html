Coverage and methodology Scope, geographic coverage…

Six DEOs; never split a shared office. Place-name lines also include Central Office, farm-to-market roads, health facilities, and irrigation.

A project is listed by the office or name in the budget line; this does not establish where it is built.

Many enacted-budget lines also name Capiz or Antique, mostly shared roads and the Panay River Basin. Each line is shown in full. Its amount is not divided between the provinces. Of each year’s enacted-budget total on this portal, that share is 28.5% in 2020, 19.2% in 2021, 28.9% in 2022, 19.8% in 2023, 14.7% in 2024, 18.4% in 2025 and 25.7% in 2026.

Among the enacted-budget lines, Aklan is named only in the nationwide Iloilo-Capiz-Aklan Expressway bundles. Those are removed by hand, for the reason published above, and they are not part of these shares.
