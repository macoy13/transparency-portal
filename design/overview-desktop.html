6 DPWH district engineering offices, serving 6 legislative districts

DPWH lists its money by the district engineering office that does the work. Each office serves one or more legislative districts, and a shared office’s money is never split between them. Representatives shown hold the seat in the 20th Congress. Representatives are listed so a reader can find their district. Nothing here establishes that a representative requested, sponsored or controlled any project or amount.
