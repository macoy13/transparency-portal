Coverage and methodology

This portal follows six DPWH district engineering offices for Iloilo Province and Iloilo City. An office’s budget is never split across the districts it serves. There is no Iloilo 5th District Engineering Office.

Neighbouring provinces: Many enacted-budget lines also name Capiz or Antique, mostly shared roads and the Panay River Basin. Each line is shown in full; its amount is not divided between the provinces.

NEP and House figures are compared, never added. Missing amounts are shown as —, never as zero.

Sources and exclusions

Figures come from the NEP FY 2027 and House Bill 10858 via the BetterGov.ph Budget API. Hand exclusions remove namesakes and nationwide PPP bundles that mention Iloilo among projects elsewhere.
